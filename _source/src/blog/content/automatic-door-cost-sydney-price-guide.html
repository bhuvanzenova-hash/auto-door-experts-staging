What Affects the Price of an Automatic Door in Sydney?

The total investment for an automatic entrance comes down to a few core factors:

  • Door Type: Automatic sliding doors are generally cheaper to install than swing doors because they require less custom structural work.
  • Size and Layout: A simple single-sliding door will cost less than a double-pane or space-saving telescopic setup.
  • Glass and Frame Selection: Custom powder-coated frames, toughened glass, or double-glazed acoustic panels all impact raw material expenses.
  • Retrofit vs. Brand New Opening: Fitting a motor onto an existing, sturdy frame is much simpler than cutting out a brand-new architectural opening.
  • Safety and Accessibility Standards: Staying fully compliant with Australian Standard AS5007 (powered doors for pedestrian access) and disability access rules dictates the types of sensors and safety backup systems required.

Let’s dive a little deeper into how these details play out on site.

1. Your Site Structure and Architectural Setup

Every building in Sydney has its own quirks. Installing commercial automatic doors in a brand-new concrete building is completely different from retrofitting a heritage building in Pyrmont or The Rocks. Header beam support, ceiling space, level flooring, and nearby electrical wiring all play a role. If your doorway needs extra steel framing or structural work to support heavy glass panels, that will naturally add to the project scope.

2. Daily Traffic and How Hard the Door Works

Not all door motors are built for the same workload. A quiet specialist clinic that sees fifty patients a day can run smoothly on a standard-duty operator. On the other hand, a busy shopping center or train station entry that opens thousands of times daily needs a heavy-duty continuous motor, reinforced tracks, and ultra-durable rollers. Putting a light-duty motor in a high-traffic location might save money on day one, but it leads to frequent breakdowns and early replacement costs later.

3. Glass Choices and Frame Style

The glass you choose has a major impact on the final look and cost. Standard commercial toughened safety glass is the classic choice for most entries. However, many modern Sydney businesses opt for double-glazed or tinted thermal glass to help keep power bills down during hot summers. Sleek frameless glass doors look fantastic in modern corporate foyers, but they require specialized mounting hardware and thicker glass compared to standard aluminum-framed setups.

4. Smart Sensors and Access Tech

Today’s automatic door installation sydney requirements go far beyond a basic motion detector. Standard microwave sensors open the door as people approach, while active safety sensors sweep the doorway area so the glass never closes on someone standing in the frame. Adding extra technology—like touchless wave sensors, keypads, or smart building access controls—will add specialized components to the installation.

5. Australian Safety Standards

In Australia, compliance isn’t optional. Automated pedestrian doors must meet strict safety guidelines under AS5007:2007 alongside disability access rules (AS1428.1). This includes mandatory safety sensors, precise calibration, and emergency battery backups that open the doors automatically during a power outage or fire alarm.

To give you a general idea: standard automatic sliding doors sit in a accessible entry range, with mid-range framed systems representing a solid middle ground for standard commercial premises. At the end of the day, there’s no single “one price fits all” figure. The only accurate number comes from a proper site assessment, not a generic price list.

The True Cost of Skipping Regular Maintenance

Choosing a cheap installation without an ongoing service plan usually ends up costing much more in the long run. Unmaintained doors have a habit of breaking down at the worst possible time—usually during peak business hours. What could have been a small, scheduled checkup turns into an urgent, costly repair.

Automatic doors carry a heavy workload every single day. The rollers roll miles along tracks, drive belts pull heavy glass continuously, and sensitive sensors deal with dust, changing weather, and direct sunlight. Skipping routine checkups often leads to:

  • Faster Wear and Tear: Worn rollers or misaligned tracks put heavy strain on the motor, leading to sudden mechanical failures.
  • After-Hours Callout Fees: When a door gets stuck wide open after business hours or jams shut on a busy afternoon, emergency service calls cost significantly more than planned maintenance.
  • Spike in Energy Bills: A door with miscalibrated sensors or sluggish closing speeds lets climate-controlled air escape, forcing your air conditioner to work twice as hard.
  • Safety Risks: Business owners are responsible for keeping automatic entryways safe. Regular sensor checks help protect your customers from unexpected bumps or accidents.

Setting up a simple regular maintenance plan keeps your entry running smoothly, protects your motor, and gives you complete peace of mind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an automatic sliding door cheaper than a swing door?

In most cases, yes. Sliding doors feature straightforward mechanical parts and widely available components, which keeps both installation and long-term maintenance costs down. However, if you already have a strong, compliant manual swing door frame in place, adding a surface-mounted automatic swing operator can often be a very cost-effective upgrade.

Is retrofitting cheaper than installing a completely new entrance?

Usually, yes. Retrofitting an automatic motor onto an existing, suitable door frame is almost always more affordable than tearing out a wall to build a new opening. It allows you to reuse existing glass panels and framing, saving on both materials and labor.

Why don’t you publish a fixed price list online?

Because accurate pricing depends entirely on your specific site—door dimensions, structural conditions, compliance requirements, and power access all change the scope. A generic online price list usually leads to unexpected extra fees once the real work begins on site.

How often should commercial automatic doors be serviced in Sydney?

Under Australian Standard AS5007, commercial powered doors should be inspected and serviced regularly—typically every three to six months depending on how often they are used. Busy retail centers and medical facilities benefit from quarterly checkups to stay reliable and fully compliant.

What’s the main difference between sensor doors and touchless doors?

Sensor doors open automatically whenever an overhead motion sensor detects someone walking toward the entry. Touchless doors require a deliberate hand wave near a proximity sensor to trigger the opening. Touchless setups are popular in sterile areas, medical spaces, and public washrooms where keeping hands off shared surfaces is a priority.
