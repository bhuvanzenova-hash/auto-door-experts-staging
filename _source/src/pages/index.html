FAQs

Straight answers.

Are your doors compliant with Australian Standards?

Yes. Every door we install or service is checked against AS 5007, with a written report for your records.

Can you automate the doors we already have?

Often, yes. Retrofit operators can be added to existing swing or sliding doors after a site inspection.

How often should automatic doors be serviced?

Twice a year for most commercial sites: once before summer, once before winter.

Do you service doors you didn't install?

Yes. We repair, service and supply parts for most automatic doors.
