FAQs

About servicing.

How often should doors be serviced?

Twice a year for most sites. Very busy entrances may need quarterly checks.

How long does a visit take?

Usually 45 minutes to 1.5 hours per door. We can come off-peak or before opening.

Will I get a record for compliance?

Yes, a written condition report after every visit.

Is it worth it for one or two doors?

Yes. One emergency callout often costs more than a year of servicing.
