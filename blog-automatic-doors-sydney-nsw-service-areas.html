Automatic Doors Sydney & NSW

21 Sep 2026 · 8 min read · The Auto Door Experts

A commercial facility manager in Parramatta called our dispatch desk with a blunt question we hear frequently: “Do your technicians actually come out this way, or is your service van parked in the Sydney CBD all day?”

It is an understandable concern. Far too many trade directories advertise “Sydney-wide” across their homepages, only for property owners in Western Sydney, the Hills District, or down on the South Coast to discover the reality: hefty travel surcharges, two-week booking delays, or technicians who simply refuse to cross the harbour.

Reliable building accessibility cannot depend on where an operator’s head office happens to sit. When an automatic sliding entrance on a busy medical centre sticks open on a rainy Monday morning, or a corporate foyer in North Sydney suffers an after-hours motor fault, you need direct answers, realistic arrival windows, and technicians equipped to resolve the issue on the spot.

Below is an honest breakdown of our day-to-day service coverage across Greater Sydney and regional New South Wales, the architectural entry solutions we deliver in each district, and how our fleet logistics keep commercial properties compliant and secure.

Sydney Metro: Fast Turnarounds for High-Density Pedestrian Traffic

From the commercial high-rises of Martin Place and Barangaroo across to dynamic retail corridors in Surry Hills and Bondi Junction, the Sydney metropolitan basin operates under non-stop foot traffic.

In dense urban environments, entrance issues immediately turn into logistical problems. A broken sliding door leaf in an office lobby traps conditioned air, compromises building physical security, and causes bottlenecks during the morning rush.

What We Deliver in the Metro Basin

  • Architectural Frameless Glass Entrances: Concealed overhead tracking and frameless 10mm to 12mm toughened safety glass panels tailored for high-end boutique retail and premium corporate foyers.
  • Touchless Activation Upgrades: Replacing legacy push buttons with hygienic, microwave Doppler motion detectors and active infrared presence curtains to improve hands-free accessibility.
  • Rapid Emergency Breakdown Service: Dedicated mobile vans positioned around major arterial routes to address trapped door leaves, snapped carriage cables, or fried control boards.

Core Sectors Serviced

  • High-street retail flagships and boutique shopfronts
  • Multi-storey corporate office foyers and co-working hubs
  • Strata residential complexes and mixed-use developments
  • Medical specialist suites and allied health clinics

Western Sydney: Heavy-Duty Industrial & Expanding Commercial Corridors

Western Sydney represents one of the most commercially active development regions in Australia. With major infrastructure investments centered around the Parramatta CBD, Western Sydney Airport, and expanding logistics parks across Western Sydney, building requirements out west differ significantly from inner-city shopfronts.

Our primary operational workshop is located in Prestons (2170). Because our base sits right alongside the M5/M7 interchange, our team does not treat Western Sydney as an afterthought—it is our home turf. Our technicians spend every day across Parramatta, Liverpool, Penrith, Blacktown, Wetherill Park, and Campbelltown.

Properties in these zones demand robust, high-capacity hardware. Industrial warehouses and retail supercentres cannot get by with delicate, light-duty door gear. They require rugged, reinforced aluminium frames that hold up against delivery trolleys, pallet jacks, and high wind exposure across open western plains.

What We Deliver in Western Sydney

  • Heavy-Duty Commercial Sliders: High-torque brushless electric motors and heavy-duty steel carriage rollers built to move wide, framed glass panels thousands of times per day.
  • Telescopic Sliding Doors: Multi-leaf sliding installations that collapse over each other, unlocking wide walkthrough access in industrial trade counters and showrooms with tight structural boundaries.
  • DDA-Compliant Swing Door Retrofits: Motorising existing hinged timber and aluminium doors on council properties, local sports centres, and community facilities.
  • Six-Monthly AS 5007 Maintenance: Routine testing, kinetic force calibration, and safety compliance certification for property groups and strata bodies.

North Shore & Northern Beaches: Precision, Healthcare & DDA Compliance

Spanning from private surgical hospitals in St Leonards and corporate offices in North Sydney up through Chatswood, Brookvale, and Mona Vale, the northern corridor presents a distinct architectural mix.

Many properties throughout the North Shore and Northern Beaches operate within established brick and concrete structures. In these settings, expanding an existing structural wall to fit a bi-parting sliding door is often cost-prohibitive or structurally impossible.

Instead, the demand centers on automatic swing door retrofits. These operators mount directly above existing manual door frames, automating accessibility without the need for destructive demolition.

Our vans cross the harbour daily. We coordinate planned work around clear booking windows so medical clinics, dental practices, and aged-care residences can maintain uninterrupted patient care while we work.

Our service perimeter does not end at the Hawkesbury River or the foot of the Blue Mountains. Through scheduled field runs and dedicated commercial project teams, we provide installation, retrofits, and contracted servicing across major regional hubs throughout New South Wales:

1. Central Coast & Newcastle

From Gosford retail centres through to large health precincts, educational facilities, and hospitality venues across Newcastle and the Hunter Region. We handle end-to-end entrance fitouts for national retail chains, regional clubs, and commercial builders requiring turnkey AS 5007 sign-offs.

2. Wollongong & The Illawarra

Servicing commercial premises down the South Coast corridor, including corporate university campuses, regional transport interchanges, and coastal manufacturing sites needing corrosion-resistant entrance automation.

Emergency Response vs. Scheduled Coverage

To maintain absolute transparency with our clients, we make an operational distinction between emergency breakdown work and scheduled projects:

  • Greater Sydney (Metro & West): We maintain rapid emergency response dispatch for urgent hazards, unsecured buildings, and high-priority retail entrance failures.
  • Regional Hubs (Newcastle, Wollongong, Central Coast): New commercial installations, major upgrades, and scheduled semi-annual maintenance contracts are managed on fixed booking dates, avoiding sudden travel surcharges and ensuring all required components arrive on site together.

What We Deliver Across Every Service Route

Whether our team is installing a bi-parting sliding glass entry for a bank in Martin Place or tuning an automated swing door in a Penrith dental clinic, our technical benchmark never wavers.

Certified AS 5007:2007 Safety Compliance

Every powered pedestrian door in Australia must comply with AS 5007:2007 (Powered doors for pedestrian access and egress). This standard establishes strict legal thresholds for closing kinetic energy, presence detection zones, and fail-safe opening during fires or power blackouts.

We do not tune doors by eye. Our technicians carry digital force gauges, safety test mats, and optical alignment sheets to calibrate your doors precisely to Australian regulatory standards, issuing a formal safety sign-off for your facility records.

Directly Employed, Brand-Agnostic Specialists

When you book a service call, you receive a dedicated automatic door technician—not an outsourced third-party handyman who only occasionally works on door electronics.

Our technicians carry factory training across all major commercial operator platforms used throughout Australian buildings, including dormakaba, Record, Besam/ASSA ABLOY, Gilgen, FAAC, and Stanley. If your door was installed by another company that has since gone out of business, we can diagnose, service, and supply replacement parts without issue.

Fully Equipped Mobile Service Units

Nothing wastes a facility manager’s budget faster than a technician who shows up, looks at a broken sensor, says “I don’t carry that part,” and bills a call-out fee anyway.

Our mobile service vans operate as rolling workshops. Every vehicle carries universal dual-technology radar/infrared sensors, heavy-duty carriage roller sets, high-tensile toothed drive belts, 24V backup batteries, floor guides, and replacement rotary key switches. We target a first-visit resolution rate on the vast majority of commercial mechanical and sensor faults.

Commercial Multi-Site Asset Management Across NSW

Managing facilities across five, ten, or thirty separate sites creates paperwork headaches when each location relies on a different local repairer.

We streamline multi-site portfolios for property groups, supermarket chains, childcare networks, and retail franchises throughout New South Wales by consolidating automatic door compliance under a single, structured agreement.

Under our commercial asset agreements, every entrance is barcoded, logged, and audited. Every six months, our technicians perform routine mechanical checks—inspecting drive belts for fraying, testing carriage bearing clearances, verifying emergency battery runtimes, and re-testing infrared safety curtains.

You receive a digital compliance log for each building, keeping your corporate risk team, insurers, and council certifiers satisfied.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover Western Sydney for emergency repairs, or just planned installations?

We provide full emergency breakdown and repair coverage throughout Western Sydney, Parramatta, and surrounding commercial precincts. With our central operations hub based in Prestons, our mobile service units are already working across the west daily, allowing us to attend to urgent faults promptly.

How do call-out fees work for regional NSW properties?

We believe in straightforward pricing. Standard metropolitan service rates apply across Greater Sydney. For projects in Newcastle, the Central Coast, or Wollongong, we provide a transparent, upfront travel and site fee before confirming your booking, ensuring you face no unexpected mileage fees or surprise charges.

Can you service an automatic door installed by a different contractor?

Yes. Our technicians service, repair, and upgrade almost every commercial automated door system on the market in Australia. We maintain extensive parts inventory for leading brands, so you do not need to hunt down the original installer to get your doors running smoothly and safely.

How often are commercial automatic doors legally required to be inspected?

Under Australian Standard AS 5007:2007, powered pedestrian doors in commercial, institutional, and high-traffic retail spaces must be inspected and serviced by a qualified technician at least once every six months. Regular servicing ensures that presence safety curtains, motor braking limits, and emergency battery fail-safes operate reliably when needed.

What information should I have ready when reporting a broken door?

To help our technicians dispatch the right components immediately, have three details handy: the style of door (single sliding, bi-parting, or swing), the brand name printed on the pelmet or key switch (if visible), and what the door is currently doing (e.g., stuck open, failing to detect approaching people, or making a scraping noise on the track).
